IRIS eye-tracking wearable

A wearable communication system that follows eye gaze, identifies the selected object, and speaks the user's likely intent.

RoleSoftware engineer
ContextENIAC XI Hackathon
Team4 members

The goal

Turn a person's gaze into a way to communicate.

IRIS was designed for people with severe motor or speech impairment. A stable gaze selects an object in the physical world, and the system translates that choice into a concise spoken suggestion.

My contribution

Designing the gaze-to-language pipeline.

  • Translated gaze fixation and image input into a focused prompt for the language model.
  • Shaped model output into a concise caregiver-facing suggestion.
  • Owned product scoping and feature prioritization within the four-person team.
  • Used dwell-time gating to control latency and unnecessary model calls.

Prototype

Look. Link. Speak.
