Scent sensor + ML classifier

A seven-week nanotechnology project combining MQ gas sensors, a BME688, and a machine-learning pipeline for gas and scent identification.

RoleTeam lead
ProgramUPenn ESAP Nanotechnology
Team3 members

The concept

Can a compact sensor learn the signature of gases and scents?

We explored how a dual-sensor system could turn raw gas resistance and concentration measurements into useful distinctions between environmental conditions and scent samples.

My work

Leading across sensor design and model training.

  • Led a three-member team building the MQ and BME688 sensing system.
  • Organized raw sensor measurements for collection and calibration.
  • Trained the BME688 using clean-air and coffee scent measurements.
  • Used MQ-135 and MQ-3 sensors to track gases in parts per million.
Gas or scent→MQ + BME688→Dataset→ML classifier→Identified class
