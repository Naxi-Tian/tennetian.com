Blood-test interpretation prototype

A decision-support platform and AI agent built to automate initial interpretation of blood-test sheets in crowded hospital settings.

RoleLead developer
Team4 members
Field settingBeijing SJS Hospital

The context

Make routine information faster to interpret.

In high-volume hospital environments, reviewing standard blood-test sheets can add to patient wait times. The team explored whether a focused AI assistant could help structure those results and surface initial guidance more efficiently.

What I led

A four-person build from document input to generated output.

  • Led development of the platform and its AI-agent workflow.
  • Designed the path from blood-test sheet input to automated interpretation.
  • Structured generated prescription suggestions as an output of the prototype.
  • Coordinated a four-week development effort across the team.

The system was conceived as decision support in a healthcare setting—not as a replacement for qualified clinical judgment.

Status

Moved from a concept into a hospital trial.

The prototype entered trial at Beijing SJS Hospital, giving the team a path to observe how the workflow performed in the environment it was designed for.
