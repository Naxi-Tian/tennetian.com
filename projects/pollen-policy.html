Beijing pollen-allergy policy

A municipal policy proposal that translated environmental research into recommendations for how Beijing regulates pollen-producing trees.

RolePolicy advisor & author
AudienceBeijing municipal government
Length9,800 words
“How can a city protect public health while respecting the ecological role of pollen?”

Research → perspective → policy

The premise

Environmental health is also a design problem.

The proposal grew from the same pollen research as the sensor work, but asked a different question: once the problem is measured, how should a city respond through planning and regulation?

The work

Translating evidence into a municipal argument.

  • Authored a 9,800-word proposal for the Beijing municipal government.
  • Connected pollen-allergy evidence with urban-tree management.
  • Framed recommendations around practical prevention and control.
  • Used a non-anthropocentric perspective to consider coexistence alongside human health.

Impact

Research that reached local policy.

The proposal contributed to the passage of a new local policy regulating pollen-producing trees—an example of technical work carrying through to a civic decision.
